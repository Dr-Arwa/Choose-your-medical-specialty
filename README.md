# 🩺 Medical Specialty Decision Matrix

A **weighted scoring decision tool** to help medical students and residents choose their ideal medical specialty based on personalized criteria and realistic assessments.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 📱 Online (No Download Required)
**Visit:** https://dr-arwa.github.io/Choose-your-medical-specialty/

Simply click the link, open in any browser, and start your decision journey!

### 💾 Offline (Download & Open)
1. Download `chooser.html` from the repository
2. Double-click the file to open in your browser
3. Works completely offline—no internet needed after download

## ✨ Features

✅ **10 Pre-loaded Criteria**
- Passion / Clinical Interest
- Ability / Personal Skills
- Opportunity / Residency Availability
- Financial Return / Stability
- Educational Environment
- Travel & International Mobility
- Social Perception / Prestige
- Patient Demographics
- Workload / Lifestyle
- Study Demand / Exam Difficulty

✅ **Customizable**
- Add your own criteria (e.g., Research Opportunities, Family Considerations)
- Set priority weights (Minor / Important / Non-negotiable)
- Rate each specialty 1-10 for realistic scoring

✅ **Detailed Results**
- Ranked specialty recommendations
- Visual progress bars showing match percentages
- Detailed scoring breakdown table
- Tie-breaker alerts for close calls

✅ **Bilingual Support**
- English & Arabic interface
- Full criteria descriptions in both languages

✅ **Privacy-Friendly**
- All calculations happen in your browser
- No data is sent anywhere
- No sign-up required

## 📋 How to Use

### Step 1: Your Shortlist
Enter 2–20 specialties you're considering. These are your baseline options to compare.

### Step 2: Criteria & Priorities
- Review the 10 default criteria
- Adjust weights for each (1 = Minor, 2 = Important, 3 = Non-negotiable)
- Add custom criteria if needed (e.g., "Research Focus")

### Step 3: Score Each Specialty
Rate each specialty from 1–10 for every criterion based on:
- Your honest self-assessment
- Realistic feedback from seniors
- Market research on the field

### Step 4: Review Results
- See ranked recommendations
- Check the detailed breakdown
- Use the "Reality Check" suggestions

## 💡 Best Practices

### Before You Start
- Talk to seniors in each specialty (at least 2–3 people)
- Research actual job market conditions
- Assess your genuine interests honestly

### While Scoring
- Base ratings on reality, not assumptions
- Consider your personal strengths/weaknesses
- Be honest about family/lifestyle needs

### After Results
1. **Verify top choices** — Interview more senior doctors
2. **Evaluate the environment** — Toxic workplace = miserable career
3. **Factor in life factors** — Family, health, commute, personal goals
4. **Don't over-optimize** — This is a guide, not destiny
